02 — From tapri to tin

The chai you grew up on. Now it travels.

Every city has that one tapri. The steel kettle that never cools, the glass that's too hot to hold, the bhaiya who knows your order.

We spent two years trying to put that feeling in a can. Real Assam leaf, cold-brewed for twelve hours, hand-pounded spices, whole milk. No powder, no shortcuts, no "chai-flavoured" anything.
