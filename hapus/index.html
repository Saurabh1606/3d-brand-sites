02 · Origin

Only one coast grows the real thing.

Laterite soil, Arabian Sea breeze and a hundred years of family orchards. Real Alphonso comes from a thin strip of Konkan coast: Ratnagiri, Devgad, Sindhudurg. Everything else is just a mango.

We work with 38 farming families who still pick by hand, with a bamboo zela net so the fruit never touches the ground.

03 · Tree to bottle in 48 hrs

We bottle May. You drink it in December.

  1. 06:00
    Picked at sunrise

    Before the heat, when the fruit is coolest and the sap is calm.

  2. Day 1–5
    Ripened in paddy straw

    The old Konkan way. No carbide, no gas, no hurry.

  3. Hour 0
    Hand-peeled & pulped

    Within hours of peak ripeness, in our unit 11 km from the orchards.

  4. Hour 48
    Flash-chilled & sealed

    Locked at −18°C so the colour, aroma and sweetness stay exactly as May made them.
